Assess

We find the truth before we move anything

We look hard at what you have: every server, application and dependency. Then we tell you what it costs to keep and what it costs to change.

  • Current system analysisWe map your infrastructure and how each piece talks to the others.
  • Workload evaluationWe measure performance and usage to see what belongs in the cloud.
  • Risk identificationWe find the weak points early and plan around them.

Plan

A roadmap built for your business, not a template

We design the target architecture and choose the platform that fits the work. Cost, capability and compliance all weigh in.

  • Platform selectionAzure, AWS or Google Cloud, matched to your workloads.
  • Target architectureA clear picture of the end state before work begins.
  • Migration roadmapPhases that keep your business running, each with a clear outcome.

Migrate

The move itself, with minimal disruption

We transfer data, move applications and cut over in a planned window. Your team keeps working while we do the heavy lifting.

  • Data transferLarge datasets move in stages and are validated before cutover.
  • ApplicationsLift and shift or modernise, whichever gives the better return.
  • Go liveA scheduled cutover with a rollback plan tested in advance.

Hybrid

Some things belong on premises. That is fine.

Not every workload should move to the public cloud. We connect your own systems with Azure, AWS or Google Cloud where it makes sense.

  • Integrated networkingSecure, reliable links between your site and the cloud.
  • Identity managementOne sign in for your team across both environments.
  • Gradual migrationMove workloads at your own pace, over months rather than all at once.

Migration is the start, not the finish.

What you gain

Cloud migration is a business decision

The technology matters because of what it does for your costs, your team and your risk.

  • Lower infrastructure costs

    Stop paying for idle servers and pay for what you use.

  • Room to grow

    Scale up for busy periods and back down when demand drops.

  • Stronger security

    Modern identity, encryption and monitoring from day one.

  • Better disaster recovery

    Automated backups and recovery plans that are tested.

  • Ongoing optimisation

    We review usage and tune the environment so you never pay for waste.

  • Someone to call

    When something breaks, you call us and we work it through to a fix.
